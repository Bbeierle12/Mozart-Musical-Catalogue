The catalogue data on this site draws on standard scholarly references. The works below are recommended starting points for further study.

Mozart

  • Köchel, Ludwig von. Chronologisch-thematisches Verzeichnis sämtlicher Tonwerke Wolfgang Amadé Mozarts. Leipzig, 1862.
  • Abert, Hermann. W. A. Mozart. Yale University Press, 2007.
  • Solomon, Maynard. Mozart: A Life. HarperCollins, 1995.
  • Sadie, Stanley. Mozart: The Early Years 1756–1781. Oxford University Press, 2006.

Bach

  • Schmieder, Wolfgang. Bach-Werke-Verzeichnis (BWV). Breitkopf & Härtel, 1950 (rev. 1990).
  • Wolff, Christoph. Johann Sebastian Bach: The Learned Musician. W. W. Norton, 2000.
  • Williams, Peter. Bach: A Musical Biography. Cambridge University Press, 2016.

Handel

  • Baselt, Bernd. Händel-Werke-Verzeichnis (HWV). Bärenreiter, 1978–86.
  • Hogwood, Christopher. Handel. Thames & Hudson, 1984.
  • Burrows, Donald. Handel. 2nd ed. Oxford University Press, 2012.

Vivaldi

  • Ryom, Peter. Répertoire des œuvres d'Antonio Vivaldi (RV). Copenhagen, 1986.
  • Talbot, Michael. Vivaldi. 2nd ed. Oxford University Press, 2000.
  • Heller, Karl. Antonio Vivaldi: The Red Priest of Venice. Amadeus Press, 1997.

General Reference

  • The New Grove Dictionary of Music and Musicians. 2nd ed. Edited by Stanley Sadie.
  • Die Musik in Geschichte und Gegenwart (MGG Online). Bärenreiter / Metzler.
  • IMSLP — International Music Score Library Project (imslp.org).
  • RISM — Répertoire International des Sources Musicales (rism.info).
