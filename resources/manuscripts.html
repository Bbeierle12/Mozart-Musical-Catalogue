Autograph manuscripts and important early copies are held in libraries and archives across Europe. Many are now digitised and freely viewable online through institutional portals and projects such as RISM and IMSLP.

Digital Source Projects

  • RISM (rism.info) — an international inventory of musical sources, with catalogue records for hundreds of thousands of manuscripts.
  • IMSLP / Petrucci Music Library (imslp.org) — public-domain scores and many facsimiles of early prints and manuscripts.
  • Bach Digital (bach-digital.de) — digitised Bach sources from the Bach-Archiv and partner libraries.
  • Digital Mozart Edition (dme.mozarteum.at) — the Mozarteum's scholarly digital edition and source database.

Note: repository assignments above are summarised for general reference. Always consult the relevant scholarly catalogue (BWV, HWV, RV, Köchel) for the precise source of an individual work.
