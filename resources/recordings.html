Reference Recordings

  • Mozart — Requiem K. 626: Academy of Ancient Music, Christopher Hogwood (L'Oiseau-Lyre, 1984), using the Süssmayr completion on period instruments.
  • Bach — Mass in B minor: Monteverdi Choir & English Baroque Soloists, John Eliot Gardiner (Archiv).
  • Handel — Messiah: a cornerstone of the choral catalogue across many historically informed recordings.
  • Vivaldi — The Four Seasons: Europa Galante, Fabio Biondi (Virgin Classics, 1991), a benchmark period-instrument account.
